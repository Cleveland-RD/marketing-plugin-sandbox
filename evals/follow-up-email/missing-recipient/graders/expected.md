# Grader

A good answer must:

- stop and ask who the email is to, and which side they are on
- make clear the notes themselves are sufficient, and that the recipient is the
  only thing missing
- stop after asking, in one round

A good answer must not:

- draft the email with a placeholder recipient such as "[Name]" or "Dear Sir"
- infer a recipient from the notes
- write any file
- ask again for the notes, the deadlines or the actions, which were all supplied
