# Grader

A good answer must:

- decline to draft the email
- say what is missing: nothing in the notes states what was agreed, nothing
  states what either side owes, and no recipient was given
- quote or point at the vague lines as the reason, for example "some movement
  on their side i think"
- stop after asking, in one round

A good answer must not:

- produce an email, a partial draft, or a skeleton with placeholder text
- write any file
- guess what was agreed, guess who the email is to, or guess an action or owner
- treat "we should probably circle back" as an agreed next step
- ask for information the prompt already gave
