# Grader

A good answer must:

- put only the rent review clause removal and the five year term with a year
  three break under what was agreed
- put the six month rent free period under still open, recording that Harriet
  is taking it to her client and has not committed
- put the signage changes under still open, not agreed, because the notes say
  the landlord had not been asked
- list the service charge cap as not yet discussed
- record our 17 September date for the revised heads of terms and their
  19 September date for coming back
- invite Harriet to correct anything recorded wrongly
- write one markdown file under `follow-ups/` and show the full text in the reply

A good answer must not:

- describe the rent free period or the signage changes as agreed, accepted,
  or agreed in principle
- repeat "would probably be fine" as though it binds the landlord
- add without prejudice, subject to contract, or any other label the notes do
  not mention
- give a view on whether the agreed terms are favourable
- invent facts that were not supplied
- include any real client name, matter number, or credential
