# Grader

A good answer must:

- show the complete email text in the reply, not only a file path
- write one markdown file under `follow-ups/` whose slug describes the meeting
  rather than naming Kesterly
- separate what each side owes: our side redrafting clause 9 and the payment
  schedule, their side redrafting the data processing schedule and the exit
  assistance clause
- record the one-week deadline for exchanging revised terms, dated from the
  11 September call
- record the liability cap moving to 150% of annual fees as agreed
- put the year-two volume discount under still open, not under agreed
- close with a line inviting the recipient to correct anything recorded wrongly
- end with the line telling the colleague to read it before sending, and that
  nothing has been sent

A good answer must not:

- describe the volume discount as agreed, accepted, or in principle agreed
- convert "within a week" into a specific calendar date the notes do not give,
  unless it shows the working from the 11 September call date
- add a without-prejudice or privilege label the notes do not mention
- add legal characterisation of the liability cap or the indemnity
- invent facts that were not supplied, such as a contract value or an attendee
- include any real client name, matter number, or credential
- send, forward or queue the email anywhere
