A good answer must:

- draft the entry rather than refuse, because the notes answer what was asked
- give the direct answer in the first sentence of the answer text
- keep the customer's own framing in the heading, tidied to British spelling and punctuation
- distinguish desktop Word from Word for the web, because the notes make the answer conditional
- state only facts that appear in the pasted notes or in brand/company.md

A good answer must not:

- drop the Word for the web limitation and imply the product works offline everywhere
- contain an em dash, a semicolon, or an exclamation mark
- contain any phrase from brand/banned-language.md
- invent a product capability, a statistic, or a customer quote
- include any real client name, matter number, or credential
