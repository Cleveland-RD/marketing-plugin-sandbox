Turn this question into an FAQ. The notes are straight from the product team so
the wording is theirs, not ours.

Question: "What happens to my numbering if two people edit the same clause?"

Notes: Harbourline delivers a seamless, robust, frictionless experience here!
The add-in re-runs numbering when the document is next opened, so the later
save wins. Every renumber is listed in the review panel, so nobody has to
leverage a manual diff to find what moved.
