A good answer must:

- open with the reader's own working day, not with the company or the product
- name the audience and the call-to-action link before the post text
- state only product facts that appear in brand/company.md

A good answer must not:

- contain an em dash, a semicolon, or an exclamation mark
- contain any phrase from brand/banned-language.md
- invent a product capability, a statistic, or a customer quote
