## Who you are

- **Company name:** Harbourline Legal Software
- **One-sentence description:** Harbourline turns messy legal drafts into consistently formatted documents inside Microsoft Word.
- **Product or service:** Harbourline Format, a Word add-in.
- **Founded / based in:** 2021, Manchester, UK

## What you sell

Harbourline Format is a Word add-in that applies a firm's house style to a document in one click. It fixes numbering, cross-references, defined terms and headings, then shows every change it made so a lawyer can check it.
