# Audiences - who you write for

> SYNTHETIC PILOT DATA for a fictional company.

### 1. Practice support lawyers

**Who they are:** Lawyers in mid-size UK firms who own templates, precedents and document quality.

**Their working day:** Fixing numbering and styles in documents fee earners send back broken, often late in the day before a deadline.

**What they care about:**
- Consistency across the firm's documents
- Not being the bottleneck before a deadline
- Tools they can trust without checking every line

**Hooks that work:**
- "The clause numbering broke again at 5.40pm."
- "Most formatting fixes are the same twelve fixes."

**Hooks that fail:**
- "AI is transforming legal documents."
- Industry jargon such as "document automation platform"

**Default CTA for this audience:**
- Book a demo: https://harbourline.example/demo

---

### 2. Law firm IT managers

**Who they are:** IT leads who approve and roll out Word add-ins across a firm.

**Their working day:** Security questionnaires, deployment tickets, and keeping Office stable.

**What they care about:**
- Central deployment and admin control
- Where document data is processed
- Not breaking Word for 200 users

**Hooks that work:**
- "Every add-in request starts with the same security questionnaire."
- "Deployment should take one policy, not 200 installs."

**Hooks that fail:**
- Anything about lawyers' productivity without saying how it is deployed

**Default CTA for this audience:**
- Requirements page: https://harbourline.example/requirements

---

## Cross-audience rule

One audience per post. Do not borrow proof points from one audience to address another.
